Dustimony™

The cleaning business app. Your bookings, your clients, and what you're owed, all in one place. Works with no signal.

  • Timed bookings: daily, weekly, every other week, monthly, or one time
  • A room-by-room checklist for every property, checked off as you clean
  • Door codes, alarm steps, and pet notes right on the job card
  • One-tap "on my way" and "running late" texts, sent from your own number
  • Invoices, payments, tips, and past-due tracking
  • Backs up to your private iCloud
  • Optional: give your crew their own sign-in, and take card payments
  • No ads, no tracking
